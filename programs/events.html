Programs · Events

Tournaments and events

Inclusive tournament formats, school championship days, community fundraisers, and corporate outings. We design the program, run the instruction, and handle the day.

Events we run

  • Inclusive tournaments: scramble and best-ball formats where golfers of every ability play on the same team
  • School championship days: the finale of a clinic series, with stations, scoring, and awards for every student
  • Community fundraisers: golf outings that raise money for a school, a cause, or Adapt2Golf's sponsored lessons
  • Corporate outings with a purpose: team-building days where your staff play alongside adaptive golfers and learn the game together
  • Veterans and first responder days: clinics and friendly competition for people who've served

What we handle

Format and rules, instruction and warm-up stations, adapted equipment for participants who need it, scoring and awards, and the schedule for the day. You bring the people. We'll work with the course on logistics and can recommend North Shore venues that are accessible for wheelchair users and golfers with limited mobility.

Why an inclusive format

A standard outing leaves a lot of people on the sidelines: the colleague who has never played, the veteran with a prosthetic, the student in a wheelchair. Inclusive formats put everyone on the course and in the game. Nobody keeps score on how far it goes. The win is that they played.

Planning timeline

Four to eight weeks is comfortable for a school day or a small outing. Fundraisers and larger corporate events benefit from more lead time to line up a course date and sponsors. Reach out early with your rough date and headcount.
