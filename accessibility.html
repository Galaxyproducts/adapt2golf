Accessibility

Accessibility isn't a page on our site. It's the whole reason Adapt2Golf exists. Here is what that means in practice.

At lessons and clinics

  • Instruction is adapted to each person's body, equipment, and learning style, not the other way around.
  • Adapted clubs, gripping aids, and standing golf carts for wheelchair users are provided at no extra cost.
  • Lessons can be taught from a seated position, with one arm, with a prosthetic, or with whatever works for you.
  • Caregivers, aides, interpreters, and family members are welcome at every lesson and event.
  • Mike adjusts how he explains things for visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners, and for students with cognitive or developmental disabilities.
  • We choose ranges and courses with accessible parking, paths, and restrooms, and we'll tell you ahead of time what to expect at a venue.
  • School clinics run in gyms and on fields, with no transportation needed.

On this website

  • Text and background colors are chosen for strong contrast.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, and focused elements are clearly outlined.
  • Images carry text descriptions for screen readers; decorative images are skipped.
  • The booking form has labeled fields and announces errors and confirmations.
  • Animation is turned off for visitors who have "reduce motion" set on their device.
  • The site works on phones and with browser zoom up to 200%.

Tell us what we missed

If something at a lesson, at an event, or on this site doesn't work for you, tell Mike directly. Call or text (847) 708-7833 or email mikesamson65@yahoo.com. We'll fix what we can and find a workaround for the rest.
