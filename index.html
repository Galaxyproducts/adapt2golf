What We Believe

Adapt. Learn. Play.

ADAPT

Every golfer is different. We adapt equipment, technique, and instruction to fit the individual — one arm, one leg, a wheelchair, or anything in between.

LEARN

We figure out how each person learns — visual, auditory, or kinesthetic — within the first five minutes. Simple, rewarding, and built around you.

PLAY

It's not about how far you hit it. It's about the fact that you did. Every swing is a victory. Every golfer who steps up is already winning.

What We Offer

Programs & Services

From one-on-one adaptive lessons to full school clinics and competitive events — we bring golf to the people who need it most.

Adaptive

ADAPTIVE GOLF

Personalized coaching for golfers with physical disabilities, amputees, wheelchair users, and anyone who needs adapted technique or equipment.

  • Fully individualized instruction
  • Adaptive equipment available
  • Standing golf carts for wheelchair users
  • All skill levels welcome
Schools

SCHOOL CLINICS

  • Small groups (1–5) or large (6–10+)
  • PE & after-school integration
  • Curriculum development available

Your Instructor

MEET MIKE

Mike Samson is a North Shore golf professional who specializes in adaptive instruction and youth development. He teaches students based on how they individually process information — auditory, visual, or kinesthetic — figuring out each person's learning style within the first five minutes.

With experience across all age groups and abilities, Mike believes that elementary and middle school students are at the perfect age to learn golf. They haven't built bad habits yet and are incredibly moldable. His approach keeps things simple, rewarding, and deeply fun.

Mike personally runs small groups of 1–5 students and brings in additional instructors for larger groups, ensuring every participant gets the attention they deserve.
