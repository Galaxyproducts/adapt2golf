Everyone
deserves a swing.

Adaptive golf instruction for people with disabilities, and inclusive golf clinics for schools, across Chicago's North Shore. Whatever you bring to the tee, we adapt the game to you.

Adaptive lessons

One-on-one and small-group instruction with adapted clubs, gripping aids, and standing golf carts for wheelchair users. Seated swings, one-arm swings, prosthetics: we build the lesson around you.

School clinics

We come to your gym or field with everything needed. Students of every ability play together in putting, chipping, and swing stations. Works as a PE unit, after-school program, or one-day intro.

Events

Inclusive tournaments, school championship days, fundraisers, and corporate outings with a purpose. We design the format, run the instruction, and handle the day.

Who we work with

  • Amputees and people with limb difference
  • Wheelchair users, with standing carts available
  • Veterans and first responders
  • Kids and students of every ability
  • Adults returning to the game after injury
  • Anyone who has never held a club
